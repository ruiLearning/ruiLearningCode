# 篇章逻辑词
1. Firstly/In the first place
2. Secondly/In the second place
3. Thirdly/Last but not least

# 常用的词或词组

### 增加/增长
- v. increase，grow(grew)，rise(rose)，climb，go(went) up，pick up，mount up，soar(高速增长)
- n. increase，growth，rise，climb，pickup，jump(跳跃增长)

### 下降/减少
- v. decrease，decline，fall(fell)，reduce，drop，grow(grew) down
- n. decrease，decline，fall，drop，slowdown，downturn

### 稳定
- v. remain,keep,stay
- adj. stable，steady，constant
- adv. much the same (几乎相同)
- n. stability
- c.g 
    - 1)Sales leveled off.
    - 2)Sales remain stable/much the same. 销售额稳定了下来。
  
### 表示程度
- very
- slowly 迟缓地，缓慢地
- dramatically 引人注目地
- rapidly 迅速地，快速地
- steadily 逐步地
- remarkably 显著突出地，出乎意外地
- steeply 急剧地
- significantly 显著地
- substantially 非常;大大地
- sharply 猛烈地；急剧地
- greatly 非常；很；大大地
- hugely 极度;极其；非常

# 常用修饰语
- 大约
roughly/approximately/about/nearly
- 变化幅度大
considerable/rapid/sharp/dramatic
- 变化幅度小
steady/gradual/slow
- 其它变化
reach a peak达到顶峰/fluctuate波动


# 句间逻辑词
### A导致B
- A contributes to /leads(led) to / brings(brought) about /result in /give rise to /cause B
- A plays a key role in /serves as a driving force for /acts as a decisive factor in /is responsible for /exerts an impact on B
- B is gathering momentum, owning to /due to A
- B is gaining popularity,largely because of A

### A对B有利
- A benefits/conduces to B
- A is beneficial to /is conducive to B
- A promotes /facilitates /enhance B
- A exerts a positive impact on B

### A对B有害
- A does harm to / poses a threat to B
- A is harmful to /detrimental to B
- A undermines/impairs/jeopardizes B
