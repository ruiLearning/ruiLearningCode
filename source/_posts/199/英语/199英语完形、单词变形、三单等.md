# 完型选项归纳
## **完型常选词**
1. `看到加粗和下划线的词先选`
2. `范围` 对立（转折&让步） > 时间 > 因果 > 递进 > 举例 > 并列
3. `对立`（转折&让步）
4. `转折` **however(4,5)** ，but，yet，nevertheless（尽管如此）
5. `让步`
    - although
    - though
    - even though(尽管)
    - even if
    - as
    - **while(4,4)**
    - whereas(然而、尽管)

6. `其它对立`
    - **against(3,4)**
    - instead(of)(1,5)
    - rather than(而不是)
    - ignoring(忽 视、忽略)
    - on the contrary(恰恰相反)
    - by contrast(相比之下)
    - in contrast(相反)(0,3)
    - otherwise(否则)(0,3)
7. `原因`
    - because
    - in that
    - now that
    - since as
    - for
    - as a result of
    - considering
    - owning to
    - due to
8. `结果`
    - so that
    - as a result
    - lead to
    - consequently
    - therefore
    - hence
    - thus
    - so
9. `举例`
    - for example
    - for instance
    - such as
    - including
    - among
    - in particular

## 出现就不选！
`虚拟语气系列`
- if only
- in case(以免万一)
- or else(否则)
- what(不放在名词后
- 不引导定语和状语## 从句)

`表示关于的词`
- with
- as to
- with regard to
- about

`条件状语从句系列`
- unless(除非)
- if


## 高频正确项
1. however 常在N1，干扰项常为therefore/otherwise/moreover/besides表递进
2. while 干扰项常为unless/until表除了
3. predict/prediction 在未来时will/would中常选
4. against 表反对，用于前后比对，干扰项常为for表支持
5. absence in the ~ of n.
6. among ~ 人
7. also strong, ~ stronger；hit, ~ hit
8. explain/emphasize 常二选一
9. equal/unequal
10. measure
11. necessary 前面有not则必选
12. before
13. ensure
14. ground ground st in st

## 高频干扰项
1. unless
2. until
3. if
4. though
5. after
6. reliable
7. deny
